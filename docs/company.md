# Este — how the company works

Este makes everyday clothing in Portugal and sells it in its own stores and online.

## Stores and warehouses
- **Stores (6):** Lisboa Chiado, Lisboa Príncipe Real, Porto Baixa, Porto Foz, Madrid Salamanca,
  London Shoreditch. London sells in pounds; the others in euros.
- **Warehouse Porto** supplies the stores.
- **Warehouse Online** in Maia ships every online order and receives every online return.

## Systems
- **Sage** runs the stores, stock in every location, purchase orders and the accounts. Portuguese
  tax rules mean the stores must sell through it.
- **Shopify** runs the online shop. It only knows the stock in Warehouse Online.
- **Notion** is the product room: one page per style with supplier, fabric, cost and prices.
- **The production plan** is a spreadsheet kept by the product team.

## Seasons
- **SS** (spring–summer) launches in March, **AW** (autumn–winter) in September.
- **Core** styles stay in the range all year, such as the Men's T-shirt.

## Who does what
- **Product team:** ranges, suppliers, the production plan.
- **Store managers:** one per store; daily sales and stock in their store.
- **Online team:** the Shopify shop, customer emails, returns.
- **Finance:** prices, margins, the accounts in Sage.
