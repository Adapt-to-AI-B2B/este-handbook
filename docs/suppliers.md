# Working with suppliers

- Most suppliers are in the north of Portugal; a few are in Spain, Italy and Turkey.
- **Lead times** run from 6 to 10 weeks; knitwear and shoes take longest. Packaging takes about 4.
- Orders for a new season go out 16 weeks before launch, so the first deliveries land 4–6 weeks
  before the season starts.
- Reorders of Core styles can go out any time. The product team checks stock every Monday.
- Never confirm a delivery date to a customer based on a supplier's lead time: lead times move.
