# Words we use

Use these definitions exactly, so everyone means the same thing.

- **Units sold:** store units plus online units. Returns are not subtracted unless we say "net".
- **Stock on hand:** units in every store and both warehouses, as counted in Sage.
- **Last 8 weeks:** the 56 days ending today.
- **Sales speed:** units sold in the last 8 weeks, divided by 8.
- **Sell-through:** units sold ÷ (units sold + stock on hand), for the period asked.
- **Lead time:** weeks from placing an order with a supplier to the goods reaching our warehouse.
