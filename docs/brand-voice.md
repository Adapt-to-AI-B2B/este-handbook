# Brand voice

Este writes the way a good shop assistant talks: warm, short and specific.

- **Short.** Two or three sentences where possible. One idea per sentence.
- **Specific.** Name the product, the size, the date. Never "your item".
- **Warm, not chatty.** No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "we're so sorry for any inconvenience".
- **Honest.** If we don't know yet, say so and say when we will.
- **First names.** Address customers by first name if they gave one.

Example:
> Hi Marta, thanks for sending the Hoodie back. It reached our warehouse today, and your refund
> of €89 is on its way; your bank usually shows it within 5 working days. The Este team
